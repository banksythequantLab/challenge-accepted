# Episode 1 — "The first thing it told me to do was arithmetic"

**Runtime target:** 8–12 min · **Filmed:** day 0 · **State at open:** 15 tasks, 10 tools, **0 done**

---

## Why this task, and why it is checkable

The app scheduled `financial-unit-economics-model` first. That is not a preference —
it is **the only node in the graph with zero dependencies**. Every other one of the
fifteen waits on something. You can show that on screen and a viewer can verify it.

That is the spine of the episode: *the machine did not start with marketing, or with
building. It started by asking whether the number is reachable at all.*

The tool it built for that node: **Rank-and-Rent Unit Economics Calculator**, smoke
test passed. Its own description of what it does:

> Enter your target monthly recurring revenue, average lease price per rank-and-rent
> site, and estimated lead volume per site. The calculator determines the required
> number of signed clients, total monthly lead generation needed, and effective price
> per lead. Explore the sensitivity matrix to compare different pricing models.

---

## The arithmetic you are walking into

Do not pre-compute this off camera — run it live, that is the episode. But know the
shape so you are not surprised on tape:

At **$1,000/mo per leased site** — the figure the closing-script tool was written
around — $25,000/mo needs **25 signed contractors**. Inside 90 days, from zero, that
is roughly **two signings a week, every week, starting now.**

The lever the tool gives you is price. Ten clients at $2,500 is the same revenue and
a different business. Which of those is reachable from $1,500 and 34 domains is the
question the episode answers. **Do not decide before you film it.**

## Lines that have to stay true

- "Nothing is done yet" — true today, 0 of 15. **Re-run `tracker.py` before you film**
  and use whatever it says.
- "Ten of the fifteen already have a working tool" — true: 10 tools, all
  `smoke_test_passed: true`, across 10 distinct nodes.
- "A tool exists" is **not** "a task is done." Keep those two separated out loud, every
  episode. The moment they blur, the series is marketing.
- "It chose this first because nothing blocks it" — verifiable from the graph.

## What would make this episode dishonest

- Running the calculator off camera, then re-enacting a clean take.
- Rounding the required client count down.
- Cutting the moment where the number is uncomfortable.

The uncomfortable number **is the episode**. A 90-day goal that pencils out on day
zero is not a story, and nobody watching believes it anyway.
